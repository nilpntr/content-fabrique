- **Beat and rep timing.** Every cut in every run landed on a beat (`beat_sync` and `off_beat_cut` pass), and every shot edge sat on a rep start, end or turn (`rep_boundaries` pass). Each hook ended on the drop. Loudness was −14.0 LUFS with a −2 dBTP true peak and music only.
- **Gino's settings take hold when set.**
  - No effects, speed ramps, fake camera moves or chapter cards appeared with his settings; the default style had all four.
  - The title came out cream, in upper case and in the `BACK 101` form.
  - Proof numbers were burned in at the top right.
  - The new `beat_grid_fixed` and `shot_reused` checks ran and caught real problems.
- **Clip feedback carries over.** After `cfctl proof never` on shot 4 of run 3 (a wide, empty shot), every later run in the workspace avoided that range, and run 7's summary said so.
- **Cost guard and credits.**
  - Each run printed its budget first and stopped at it.
  - `cfctl eval suite` printed its worst case and checked it against `TEST_SPEND_CAP_MICROS`.
  - Credits were charged for successful edits (6 each) and released for failed ones. The ledger in `cfctl credits show` matches the runs.
- **Footage handling.** Once the sandbox could write its output (§5), `cfctl footage check` rated the clips usable. It noted the rotation and the variable frame rate, and read the sound in spite of the extra Apple spatial-audio track. Five 4K clips ingested in about 8 minutes on one capped sandbox.

## 4. What broke, with evidence

### 4.1 The title can never sit on the posing hook

**Evidence.**
- In all seven edits that produced a version (runs 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 10 and 11), the renderer moved the title off the opening pose, to between 1.1 s and 4.2 s, onto a row shot.
- Every check report gives the same reason, for example: "t1 has no room clear of the person's body at 0-3000 ms (the head starts at y 346 (18 % from the top) and the clear band above it is 106 px tall) … tried: above the head (a band of 0 px), beside it (at most 310 x 0 px of background) and below the person, down to the smallest legible scale 0.56".
- Run 11 asked for the title on the pose in so many words. Sonnet tried, and the tool refused every placement.

**Why it is a placement bug.**
- A frame of `posing.mov` (6 s) shows plain dark wall above the head: the top of the head is about 20 % down the frame.
- The top text safe zone takes most of that band, and the lats and arms make the body box too wide for the title to fit beside him.
- A one-line title about 80 px tall would fit in 106 px; the renderer refuses even at scale 0.56.

**What it costs.**
- `hook_title`, `format_followed` and `hook_score` fail.
- The title collides with chapter cards (run 1) and lands on busy backgrounds: on the red ceiling lamps in runs 3 and 7, and touching the top of the head in run 7.
- Gino's "… 101" format opens on its title, so every one of his openings breaks.

### 4.2 Extra careful (max) edits burn their whole budget and make nothing

- **Run 6 (first edit):** 8 Opus calls at effort max, 4 of them with 13.6k to 18.6k output tokens (mostly thinking). The last was cut off at max_tokens. It spent $2.00 of $2.00 and produced 0 timeline versions; `plan_edit` was reached once.
- **Run 5 (revision):** 5 calls, the last 13.6k tokens cut off. It spent $0.80 of $0.80 and produced no version.
- The credits were released, but the money is spent: $2.80 here for nothing.
- `config/plans/2026-10c.yaml` assumes a max edit costs about $1.01. On five 4K clips plus a song it does not fit in $2.00.

### 4.3 A standard Opus revision on the creator plan cannot start

- Run 4 failed with `budget` after 335 ms, with 0 calls and $0.00 spent. The creator plan gives a revision $0.32, and the first call's worst case is larger:
  - about 60k tokens of context as a cache write at $5/M, which is about $0.30
  - plus 12,000 `max_tokens` at $20/M, which is $0.24
- So `llm` refuses the call (`CapRun`) every time, on any project of this size.
- The same revision on Sonnet (run 11) fits: $0.29 of $0.32. With `v1-start`, the default for creators, revisions are dead.

### 4.4 The editor finishes while Gino's rule checks still fail

- **Run 3** finished with `beat_grid_fixed` failing: the last shot was 10 beats, not 4.
- **Run 10 (live eval)** finished with `shot_reused` failing: each of the four tripod clips was used 4 times, in 15 one-second shots that cycle the same angles. Gino's rule is that a tripod clip gives one shot.
- **The cause:** both checks only advise. A creator who set `cut_grid: fixed_2` or `shot_reuse: once` asked for a rule, not a preference.
- **The 2-beat grid adds a conflict.** At 116.5 BPM it means 1.03 s shots. With one shot per tripod clip, four rep clips give about 5 s of video. The editor has to know that it may hold a rep for 4, 6 or 8 beats (Gino: "reps may be longer than 2 beats, the cuts stay on the beat"). Runs 7 and 8 found that; run 10 did not.

### 4.6 "Concentric only" is not what `full_reps` does

- Gino's rule 5 (show the lift, never the lowering) is still missing, as `gino-edit-rules.md` item 4 says.

## 5. Setup problems found (fixed in docs or noted)

1. **Rootless Docker breaks the media sandbox.**
   - `internal/sandbox/docker.go` runs every tool as the worker's own uid (`--user 1000:1000`). Rootless Docker maps that uid to a subordinate uid, which cannot write the job's output directory.
   - Every ffmpeg step fails with `Permission denied` (exit 243).
   - `cfctl footage check` then told the creator that the sound of all five clips "can't be read" and that the song is "not usable … Try exporting it again". That is wrong: the files were fine.
   - Workaround: a `docker` shim that rewrites `--user` to `0:0` (container root is the unprivileged host user there; see docs/06). The real fix is in §6.
2. **The footage check hides infrastructure errors as file problems.** A sandbox that cannot write gives the same message as a broken file (`toolFailed` makes `SoundUnreadable`). A failure such as "permission denied", or the output missing on every file, should be an error, not a verdict on the creator's footage.
3. **The worker's health server listens on `:8081`, every interface.** On a shared box, use `-health-addr 127.0.0.1:8081`. A loopback default for `CF_ENV=local` would be safer.
4. **No headless way to make a workspace.** It takes a Clerk sign-in, so the VPS test seeded a user, a workspace and a membership with SQL. A `cfctl workspace create --dev` would remove that step.
5. **The media-tools build takes the whole box.** It runs `make -j$(nproc)` for 15 minutes. On a shared box, build it in a BuildKit container with a CPU quota; `cpuset` is ignored under rootless Docker. This is in docs/06.
6. **Creator data is tracked in git.** `suites/back-day.local.yaml` and five files under `eval-results/` are in the repository although `.gitignore` excludes them; they came in with the merge 8f5671d. They hold workspace, asset and project ids and scores. Untracking them (`git rm --cached`) would also delete the local copy on every checkout that pulls the change, so back the file up first. It was not done in this PR for that reason.
7. **`cfctl style` output.** `style set` does not print `cut_grid` and `shot_reuse` in its list of settings, though it stores them. `style show --json` prints `null` when there is no profile, even if settings exist.
8. **The VPS database starts empty**, so `cfctl spend summary` there does not see the founder's earlier spend. The session budget was set as `TEST_SPEND_CAP_MICROS=18000000` in the VPS `.env`.

## 6. Ranked fixes

1. **Hook title placement (§4.1).**
   - Let a hook title use the band above the head down to a small margin, overriding the top safe zone for the hook only.
   - Try one line before giving up at scale 0.56.
   - Let `plan_edit` and `find_moments` prefer pose moments with room above the head.
   - Never move a hook title onto another shot without telling the creator at plan time.
   - Deterministic Go, $0. Proof: run 7's project re-checked, and a `title-on-hook` feedback item in `suites/feedback.yaml`.
2. **Max effort runaway (§4.2).**
   - Give effort max a thinking budget per call (for example 6k).
   - Make the runtime require an `edit_timeline` before half the budget is spent; the `OnLimit` path should keep a draft.
   - Until then, hide "extra careful" or route it to a cheaper first pass.
3. **Revisions under the plan budget (§4.3).** Any one of these:
   - Shrink the first call's `max_tokens` to fit the remaining budget, as the cut-off path already does.
   - Route creator-plan revisions to Sonnet 5.5.
   - Raise `edit.revision` to cover a worst case of about $0.55.

   Add a test that a revision on a 5-clip project with a song can start under each plan's budget.
4. **Make the creator's rule checks hard (§4.4).** When `cut_grid` is `fixed_2` or `shot_reuse` is `once`, `beat_grid_fixed` and `shot_reused` join the hard constraints: they escalate and block `finish`. Also tell the planner that rep shots may be 4, 6 or 8 beats.
5. **Align the eval suite with the creator (§4.5).**
   - Gate `no-dead-static-takes` and the motion items on the creator's settings.
   - Score a stored version against the style that was in force when it was made (keep a snapshot with the version).
   - Restore Gino's references (`references_dir`) so `style_match` means something.
   - Add Gino's rules as feedback items with detectors.
6. **Sandbox under rootless Docker, and an honest footage check (§5.1, §5.2).**
   - Detect rootless Docker once at start (`docker info --format '{{.SecurityOptions}}'` has `name=rootless`) and run the container as `0:0` there; or add a `MEDIA_SANDBOX_USER` override.
   - Make the footage check return an error when the tool could not write its output.
7. **Concentric cut timing (§4.6).** The up and down halves of each rep in the analysis, a `cut_timing: concentric` setting, and a `concentric_shown` check. This is item 4 of `gino-edit-rules.md`.
8. **Developer ergonomics.** `cfctl workspace create --dev`; a loopback health address by default when local; `cfctl style set` and `style show` printing every setting.
9. **Untrack creator data (§5.6)**, after the founder backs up `suites/back-day.local.yaml`.
10. **Model routing.** On this footage Sonnet 5.5 matched or beat Opus on Gino's rules at 35 to 55 % of the cost. Benchmark `dev-mid` against `v1-start` on the back-day suite once fixes 1 and 5 make the suite trustworthy.
